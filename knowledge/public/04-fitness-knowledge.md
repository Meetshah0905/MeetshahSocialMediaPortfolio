# Meet Shah — Educational Fitness Knowledge

## Training Fundamentals
1. **Form Precision**: Prioritizing full range of motion, controlled eccentrics, and scapular stability over heavy weights with poor technique.
2. **Compound Movements**: Squats, deadlifts, overhead presses, pull-ups, and bench presses form the core of progressive overload programming.
3. **Consistency**: 3–5 structured sessions per week with adequate recovery yield superior long-term results compared to sporadic extreme intensity.

## Vegetarian Nutrition Framework
1. **Protein Targets**: Aiming for 1.6–2.2g of protein per kg of body weight for muscle hypertrophy.
2. **Vegetarian Sources**: Tofu, paneer, Greek yogurt, lentils, legumes, whey protein isolate, and soy chunks.
3. **Macro Tracking**: Balancing complex carbohydrates for energy and healthy fats for hormonal health.

## Common Fitness Misconceptions
- **Myth**: Spot reduction burns belly fat directly.
  - **Fact**: Caloric deficit drives systemic fat loss across the entire body.
- **Myth**: Lifting weights makes beginners bulky overnight.
  - **Fact**: Hypertrophy requires sustained progressive overload, caloric surplus, and time.
