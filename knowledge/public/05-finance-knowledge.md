# Meet Shah — Educational Finance Knowledge

## Core Personal Finance Principles
1. **Emergency Fund First**: 6 months of living expenses placed in high-liquidity accounts before investing in volatile assets.
2. **Automated Savings**: Transferring a fixed percentage of income directly into Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) on salary day.
3. **Term Insurance & Health Cover**: Securing adequate term life insurance and comprehensive health insurance before buying market instruments.

## Educational Investing Framework
1. **Index Funds & Long-Term SIPs**: Broad market exposure (e.g., Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50) for long-term compound growth.
2. **Business Model Analysis**: Evaluating companies based on revenue models, competitive moats, cash flows, and management integrity rather than short-term price noise.
3. **Risk Management**: Diversifying across asset classes (equity, debt, gold) based on individual risk tolerance and time horizon.
