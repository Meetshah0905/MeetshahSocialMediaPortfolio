# Meet Shah — UGC & Video Production Framework

## High-Retention Video Anatomy (0-60s)
1. **0–3 Seconds (The Hook)**: Visual or verbal pattern interrupt designed to stop feed scrolling (e.g., text overlay, movement, unexpected question).
2. **3–30 Seconds (Pain-Point & Demonstration)**: Introducing a relatable issue in fitness or finance and demonstrating the solution through hands-on use.
3. **30–60 Seconds (Trust & Clear Call-to-Action)**: Presenting proof, features, disclaimers, and a single, direct next step (link, code, application).

## Production Quality Standards
- **Resolution**: 4K / 1080p vertical format (9:16 optimized for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok/Meta ads).
- **Audio**: Crisp lapel microphone audio with balanced background score.
- **Graphics**: Custom animated text callouts, motion graphics, and brand-safe color grading.

## Ad Performance Strategy
- **Hook Testing**: Providing 3 distinct opening hook variations for a single ad body to maximize creative testing efficiency in ad accounts.
- **Call-to-Action Options**: Custom promo codes, app download prompts, landing page links.
