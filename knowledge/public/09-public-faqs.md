# Frequently Asked Questions (Public FAQs)

### Q1: Who is Meet Shah?
Meet Shah is a content creator based in Ahmedabad, India, specializing in Fitness (`@meetsofficial`) and Personal Finance (`@meet.fitfix`).

### Q2: Is Meet Shah a SEBI registered investment adviser?
No. All financial content provided across reels, videos, and portfolio walkthroughs is strictly for educational and informational purposes. It does not constitute individual investment advice or stock recommendations.

### Q3: How can a brand collaborate with Meet Shah?
Brands can submit a campaign proposal through the official proposal form on the website (`/contact?vertical=brand`). Meet reviews proposals for sponsored reels, integrated videos, and UGC performance ad packages.

### Q4: Can I book a 1:1 consultation session with Meet Shah?
Yes, when slots are open. Strategy sessions for 1:1 Fitness coaching or 1:1 Personal Finance frameworks can be booked via the official meeting booking page (`https://cal.com/meetshah/30min` or the meeting action link).

### Q5: How do I join Meet Shah's creator team?
Meet Shah hires video editors and videographers on an ongoing basis. Candidates can submit their portfolio and application via the Creator Team page (`/join-creator-team`).

### Q6: Does Meet Shah offer personalized workout or meal plans in chat?
The AI assistant provides general educational guidelines on training form and vegetarian nutrition. For 1:1 personalized coaching structures, book a 1:1 strategy session.
